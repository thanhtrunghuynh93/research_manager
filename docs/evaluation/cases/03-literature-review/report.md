# Week of 14–20 September — Survey of uncertainty estimates

## Planned work
Read and classify the post-hoc calibration literature; produce a comparison table.

## Work performed
Read 14 papers, classified them by whether they require a held-out set, whether they are
distribution-free, and what guarantee they claim. Wrote the comparison table and a two-page
synthesis of where the guarantees actually differ.

## Results and research learning
The distinction that matters for us is not "Bayesian vs frequentist" but whether the method needs
exchangeability. Six of the fourteen assume it silently. Our deployment has temporal drift, so four
of the methods I had shortlisted are inapplicable, which I had not realised before doing this.

## Evidence
The comparison table and the synthesis note. No code this week; this is a reading week by plan.
